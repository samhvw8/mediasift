# mediasift

A sensor for agents that cannot watch or hear: `mediasift see VIDEO` writes a timeline card (picture events, camera moves, shots, sounds, speakers, speech, on-screen text) plus ~1 fps frame sheets. The v0.2 segmentation, keyframes and search are still available.

* [Analysis pipeline](architecture/pipeline.md) - modules, data flow, outputs.
* [decisions/](decisions/index.md) - why the heuristics are what they are, with the measurements.
* [benchmarks/](benchmarks/index.md) - how mediasift is measured and its current scores.

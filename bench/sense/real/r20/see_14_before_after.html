Executive Summary: Why `see-13` Missed Brief Gestures & Fast Pans

An AI agent reading `mediasift see` cannot watch continuous video; it sees only the timeline card (`card.md`), `~1 fps` contact sheets (`flow_NN.jpg`), and on-demand zoom strips (`mediasift look`). Before `see-14`, two structural blind spots caused misses on real phone videos and camera-motion benchmarks.

Before (see-13)

Two Implicit Assumptions That Failed in Practice

  • Unshifted `(x, y)` block differences + 0.5 s whole-frame FFT: When the camera panned, every background block changed coordinates and flooded `diff`. When the camera was still but a person moved in the center (`mb_still_1`), whole-frame phase correlation locked onto the person and reported a false camera tilt. On fast pans (`mb_pan_1`), a `0.5 s` lag wrapped around the FFT window and missed the pan entirely (`1/3` recall).
  • Rigid `(k + 0.5)` clock midpoints + full-frame `look` tiles: Both `flow_sheets()` and `look()` sampled frames on a uniform clock. In `reel_en.mp4` (`q06`), the speaker raised his fist to his mouth for `0.25 s` (`9.13s–9.38s`). Uniform sampling captured `8.90s` and `9.47s`, stepping right over the gesture. Even when a frame landed near a gesture, a `320×568` portrait tile rendered a hand or corner prop only ~35 px wide.
After (see-14)

Codec-Aligned Picture Sensing (0 Neural Weights, $0/mo)

  • 1. Outer 25% Border-Strip Consensus + Frame Warp (`events.build_grid` & `camera_moves`): Measures 1-frame phase correlation on the 4 outer border strips (`Top, Bottom, Left, Right`), takes their median shift `(dx, dy)`, and warps `prev` before computing `diff` and `pix`. Camera motion is separated from foreground motion (`3/3` pan recall, `0/3` still FP, `−40.2%` background noise).
  • 2. Stratified Intra-Bucket Peak Snapping (`events.select_bucket_peaks`): Keeps strict 1-tile-per-bucket coverage (no timeline gaps), but snaps each bucket within its interior `[0.18, 0.82]` window to its highest motion-compensated residual peak (`> 1.35×` median), staying at the midpoint near scene cuts (`0.6 s` guard).
  • 3. Peak-Snapped `look` + Active-Region Codec Canvas Zoom Row (`see._active_crop_row`): Snaps `look` timestamps to `0.25 s` `frame_peaks` and appends a 4-tile `2×` zoomed crop row of the active gesture/UI bounding box inside the same `look_*.jpg` sheet (`0` extra image reads).

Intuition 1: Outer Border-Strip Consensus vs. Whole-Frame Correlation

Inspired by codec motion vectors (`OneVision-Encoder` / `HEVC` prediction): a real camera pan or tilt shifts the entire outer frame border coherently, whereas a person gesturing or dancing occupies the center while the background border stays still.

Intuition 2 & 3: Stratified Intra-Bucket Peak Snapping + `look` Codec Canvas Zoom Row

Why did `reel_en.q06` (*"When he says 'stay up late doing nothing', what is his hand doing?"*) fail in `r19` and succeed in `r20`? Because a `0.25 s` hand gesture falls between uniform clock midpoints, and needs both temporal peak alignment and spatial 2× active-region cropping.

Mathematical Formulation & Production Implementation

1. Border-Consensus Camera Shift & Residual Warp (`src/mediasift/events.py`)

For each 15 fps frame of size 144×256, we extract the four 25% border strips (T, B, L, R) and compute FFT phase correlation against the previous frame: 

def _border_shift(prev_y: np.ndarray, cur_y: np.ndarray) -> tuple[float, float, float]:
    h, w = cur_y.shape
    bh, bw = max(16, h // 4), max(16, w // 4)
    strips = (
        (prev_y[:bh, :], cur_y[:bh, :]),
        (prev_y[h - bh :, :], cur_y[h - bh :, :]),
        (prev_y[:, :bw], cur_y[:, :bw]),
        (prev_y[:, w - bw :], cur_y[:, w - bw :]),
    )
    dxs, dys, pks = [], [], []
    for a, b in strips:
        dx, dy, pk = _shift(a, b)
        dxs.append(dx); dys.append(dy); pks.append(pk)
    return float(np.median(dxs)), float(np.median(dys)), float(np.median(pks))

When `med_pk >= 0.16` and step displacement is at least `1 px`, `prev_f` is warped by `(dx, dy)` before computing block color differences (`diff`) and luma differences (`pix`).

2. Cut-Guarded Intra-Bucket Peak Selector (`src/mediasift/events.py`)

Each bucket [b0, b1] checks whether a scene cut (`chg_frac > 0.40`) is within 0.6 * max(step, 1.0) seconds of the midpoint. Otherwise, it searches [b0 + 0.18*step, b1 - 0.18*step] for the maximum motion-compensated residual activity: 

for k in range(n):
    b0, b1 = start + k * step, start + (k + 1) * step
    mid = b0 + 0.5 * step
    if len(cut_times) and np.min(np.abs(cut_times - mid)) < 0.6 * max(step, 1.0):
        out.append(round(mid, 2))
        continue
    mask = (ts >= b0 + 0.18 * step) & (ts <= b1 - 0.18 * step)
    if mask.any():
        sub_t, sub_a = ts[mask], act[mask]
        pk_idx = int(np.argmax(sub_a))
        if sub_a[pk_idx] > max(0.02, 1.35 * float(np.median(sub_a))):
            out.append(round(float(sub_t[pk_idx]), 2))
            continue
    out.append(round(mid, 2))

## Builds
### m08.r1.1 Replay flags: count the play once, however many times it is shown
Builds on: m08.7 + m02.7 + m05.4
Broadcast loggers mark replays so a goal shown four times counts as one goal. Join m02.7's exact-frame repeats (grid hashes already in memory) and m05.4's exact-copy sound spans (log-mel frames already computed) into one `replay_of` field on each event in `sensed.json`, and have `mediasift log --count` report both "shown 4 times" and "happened once". That fixes the count questions where loops and recycled B-roll fool the agent today. Nothing goes on the card unless a counted line covers a replay. Judge: synthetic clips with known loops and spliced-in copies, scored on "how many times did it happen" against "how many times is it shown".

### m08.r1.2 Phases of play: split the video into continuous events, not shots
Builds on: m05.3 + m08.2 + m02.5
An analyst divides a match into phases, not camera angles. Where m05.3's sound bed carries across a cut, the cut stays inside one phase. Where the bed resets, a new phase starts. Each phase then gets the m08.2 who-holds-the-floor chain: "phase 2, 12.0-31.5: one event, 6 angles, voice A asks, B answers". That answers "how many separate incidents are in this compilation" and "which shots show the same moment". m05.3 and m02.5 gave a cut-by-cut word. This build turns it into a count the agent can query with `log --kind phase`. Judge: a single continuous take cut into shots, compared with the same shots taken from different takes, plus hand-labelled phases on the 6 real videos.

### m08.r1.3 Team sheet with a second opinion: run the face pass only where the cheap witnesses disagree
Builds on: m08.3 + m02.8 + m05.5
My voice-to-shot binding costs nothing but is weak on two-shots: voices C and D share shot E in Tears of Steel. m02.8's mouth correlation is strong but costs Vision landmarks at 10 fps. m05.5's pan is free, but only on stereo files. Run the witnesses in cost order, the way an analyst confirms identity with a second camera angle only when the shirt number is hidden. Bind from the shot first, settle shared shots with pan, and run face landmarks only on the remaining contested spans, so the expensive pass covers seconds, not the whole video. Print "voice D = face in shot E" only when two witnesses agree. A disagreement goes to `log` as disputed. Judge: "who is speaking on screen" questions on the real bench, plus the Vision-seconds spent per video as a cost number.

### m08.r1.4 One match clock across many cameras: mains hum syncs different phones
Builds on: m02.1 (pushed further) + m08.7
m02.1 reads the grid hum to find a place and a splice inside one file. The bolder use: every recording made on the same grid at the same moment carries the same tiny wander in mains frequency, so the hum trace works as a shared stadium clock. `mediasift sync A.mp4 B.mp4` cross-correlates the two ENF traces from a narrow band-pass at 50 or 60 Hz on the existing 16 kHz audio, which is microseconds of CPU. It prints "B starts 41.2 s after A; recorded at the same time", or "no overlap: not the same moment". That gives fact-checks a way to tell whether two viral angles show one incident, gives `log` a merged multi-angle timeline, and needs no network. Judge: two phones filming together under mains lighting, then the same clips with one shifted by a known offset or taken from another day.

### m08.r1.5 Provenance box score: one header line for how the video was made
Builds on: m08.5 + m05.1 + m05.2 + m05.6 + m02.6
A match report opens with a line of stats. Fold the strongest m05 and m02 measurements into one header line of about 35 tokens instead of a word on every line: "layers: voice A (ceiling 12 kHz, room 1, centred) over music (19 kHz, wide, ducks 15 dB); 0 s location sound; CapCut, 3rd generation". It prints only the fields that differ from the plain "one recording, one room" case, so most videos get no line. The re-voice job gets its target gain and ducking from the line without a separation pass, and a fact-check gets the made-in-the-edit verdict before reading a single `say` line. Judge: the m05 synthetic remixes, graded on the header fields alone, plus 6 "was this narrated later / recorded apart" questions on the real reels.

### m08.r1.6 Scout first, report later: every new sense lands in `log` before it can reach the card
Builds on: m08.7 + m05 (all) + m02 (all)
Clubs scout everything but print only the stats the coach keeps asking for. All of m02's and m05's new measurements (ceilings, pans, room letters, splice marks, copies, ENF) go into `sensed.json` and are reachable through `mediasift log`. They cost zero card tokens and need no card-level bench to ship. The existing session-log mining (61 recorded `look` zooms) gets a twin: count which `log` fields agents actually query. A field earns a card line only when a bench question needs it, and agents query that field often. This turns "every sense needs a judge before it reaches the card" from a gate on building into a promotion ladder, and the query counts are the evidence. Runs: one more field family in `sensed.json`, and nothing new on the card.

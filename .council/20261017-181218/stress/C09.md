# Stress test: C09 Check the agent's own deliverable before it says done

What I checked on disk before the attack:

- The owner quotes are real. They come from the 8-lens council's m04 file, which cites session prompts under `~/.ccp/profiles/shared/projects`: rushed and shifted HSK audio (09-30), "slides, not film" (09-23), the crashed 2-hour demo (10-15/16). Each complaint happened **once**, and each is a different job.
- The HSK evidence is real. `transcribe_islands.py` line 3 says Whisper's segment drift "shifted every clip by one item". `tts.py` line 3 names the slurred browser voices, and line 11 sets `zh-CN-XiaoxiaoNeural` at `-15%`. Both scripts were last changed on 2026-09-30. The project has no git commits, so no history shows whether TTS work ever comes back.
- The label set is harder than the concept assumes. `dist/hsk2/tts_needed.json` has 916 labels, and **46% are 1 or 2 Han characters (212 single characters)**. In hsk3 it is 52% of 1,279 (221 single characters). Labels hold punctuation and parenthesised particles (`"! 太棒了！"`, `"(呢)"`), 65 of them in parentheses.
- The intent from disk is already out of date. `data/hsk2/tts` has 1,103 files, but 188 of them have no label in `tts_needed.json`. In hsk3, 180 of 1,458 have no label.
- `clips.json` holds spans only (172 + 314 word spans, 2 + 2 of them `None`). The span text must come from the `data_L*.json` lesson files by position. That is a second place where an off-by-one can enter.
- One prior-art link is dead: the NVIDIA Riva TTS evaluation tutorial returns 404 (https://docs.nvidia.com/deeplearning/riva/user-guide/docs/tutorials/tts-evaluate.html). The dev.to QC post (https://dev.to/orca_forge/defects-missed-in-transcription-ai-speaks-after-05-second-silence-3id5) and vllm-omni-mlx issue #37 (https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/vllm-omni-mlx/issues/37) are live and say what C09 claims.

## Fragile assumptions

Ranked from most fragile to least.

**1. Untested: the same faults will come back, in the same jobs.** Every job word comes from one incident. HSK TTS ended on 09-30, and its two bugs were fixed by changing the voice and the alignment method. The p5 "slides" video and the crashed demo each happened once. No log shows a second TTS batch, a second join, or a second rejected animation. The concept's own upkeep rule says a job word needs "a recorded owner complaint". Today each job has exactly one, and the bench for each job is that same incident. A check tuned on the one incident it was built from will pass its own bench almost by construction. The real question is whether the next deliverable is a TTS batch at all. The re-voice dub is the most likely next job, and it is a different shape: lines in time slots, not isolated clips.

**2. Shaky: per-item ASR can judge clips of 1 or 2 syllables.** About half the HSK labels are 1 or 2 characters. A 0.3 to 0.6 s clip of one Mandarin syllable is where Whisper is weakest. With no context, it either returns nothing or invents a stock phrase. The Whisper discussion on hallucination says short isolated chunks "are more likely to lose context and hallucinate near boundaries" (https://github.com/openai/whisper/discussions/1606). Toneless pinyin makes this worse for the shift check, not better. Neighbouring single-syllable labels collide often (`shi`, `yi`, `ji`), so "best match is label *i+1*" turns into ties. The 2% false-alarm kill line is the right test. But it is likely to fail on the single-character half, and the concept has no plan for that case.

**3. Shaky: an energy envelope can measure "stuck together" as a gap in milliseconds.** De Jong and Wempe count syllable nuclei to compare speech **rate** across speakers and tasks (https://doi.org/10.3758/brm.41.2.385; summary at https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Praat-script-to-detect-syllable-nuclei-and-measure-Jong-Wempe/797241af32e8684a106cc7bc38e3a565c63ae52a). They were validated on Dutch. They do not measure the silent gap between two syllables. Inside a natural Mandarin disyllable such as 银行, there is often no silent gap at all, only an intensity dip. So "gap < 30 ms, textbook median 90 ms" may measure something that does not exist in good speech either. The concept marks both numbers as placeholders, which is honest. But the unit itself may be wrong, not only the threshold.

Also checked, and solid:

- Comparing the file with the agent's own intent is sound. The dev.to post shows that ASR alone missed 4 of 12 defective voices, and an RMS envelope found them. This supports C09's choice to pair ASR with envelope checks.
- `--job summary` link checking is pure text over cached `sensed.json`. It is cheap and has a clear answer key.

Blind spot the concept does not name: **wrong tones and wrong polyphone readings**. For a Chinese learning site, these are the faults that matter most. Edge TTS can read 行 as *xíng* in 银行. Whisper will still write 银行, because its language model knows the word. Toneless pinyin removes tone by design. A green `accept tts` table therefore says nothing about the fault a learner is most hurt by. The agent will still read it as "the audio is right".

## Pre-mortem

It is 2026-10-17 a year from now. `accept` exists, and nobody uses it.

The most probable story is ordinary. The First move ran on HSK2. The injected +1 shift was found at clip 30, because neighbour matching on multi-character labels works well. The rate check split `+30%` from `-15%`. The false-alarm rate on shipped clips came out near 5%, almost all on single-character labels. The developer excluded items under 2 syllables, and the numbers then passed. `accept --job tts` shipped with a SKILL.md rule.

After that, the agent did not produce another 300-clip TTS batch. The next media jobs were a Vietnamese re-voice (lines in time slots, with background audio), two p5 explainers, and a screen recording. None of them matched the `tts` job word cleanly. The agent skipped `accept`, or ran `--job tts` on the dub and got `heard text` failures on every line that ran over its slot. `clip`, `join` and `summary` never got benches, because no second complaint came in to build one from. They stayed listed in the plan and absent from the code.

Then the whisper pin was bumped for roadmap step 2. One canary, a 1.5x stretch on a random 2-syllable item, stopped failing the rate check. `accept` began to exit 3 ("judge broken") on about one run in five. This was correct by design. The agent learned that exit 3 is noise and that a rerun usually goes green. After two such sessions the owner removed the SKILL.md line to save tokens. `usage.jsonl` shows 6 `accept` runs in 12 months, all in October.

Nothing broke loudly. The tool kept its promise to crash rather than corrupt. It was simply aimed at last month's fault.

## Fixes

| # | weakness | smallest change that fixes it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Job words built on one incident each, never re-tested | Ship one job only: `tts`, renamed by shape to `--job clips` ("items vs labels"), so a dub's line list and a TTS batch use one path. Remove `clip`, `join` and `summary` from the plan. Each one enters only when a **second** real incident of that job appears in the session logs, and the bench uses that second incident, not the one the check was designed on. |
| 2 | Single-syllable clips break free ASR and toneless-pinyin matching | Do not transcribe freely and then compare. **Score the candidate labels instead**: teacher-force whisper on labels *i-2 .. i+2* for each clip and rank them by log-likelihood. This is the question the shift check asks. It cannot invent "谢谢观看", and it works on one syllable. Cost: 5 short decoder passes per clip, still seconds per batch. Keep free ASR only to quote what was heard for items of 3 or more syllables. Add a kill line: the false-alarm rate is reported **separately** for 1-syllable, 2-syllable and 3+-syllable items, and no single group may exceed 2%. |
| 3 | Rate and gap checks are undefined on very short items | Apply `rushed` only to items of 4 or more syllables (in HSK2 that is about 46% of labels, so the check still has a large sample). Replace "gap in ms" with **dip depth between adjacent nuclei relative to the lower peak**, and pick the unit only after one plot of textbook vs shipped vs `+30%`. If dip depth does not split them, drop `joined`, and record the number. |
| 4 | Canaries can fail for reasons unrelated to a broken judge | Seed canary selection from the batch hash, so a rerun gives the same result. Pick each canary only from items eligible for its check (4+ syllables for `rushed`, 2+ for `joined`, distinct toneless pinyin from the neighbour for the label swap). If no item is eligible, print `rushed: skipped, no item with 4+ syllables`. Do not exit 3 in that case. Exit 3 stays reserved for "an eligible canary passed". |
| 5 | Tones and polyphone readings are unchecked, and a green table hides that | No fix with the pinned models: this is the price. Make it visible in 3 words. The header line of the box score lists what was **not** checked: `accept clips: 300 items (zh); not checked: tones, voice quality`. SKILL.md says that `accept` checks text, order, pace and pauses, and does not check pronunciation. |
| 6 | The intent from disk is stale (188 files without labels in hsk2, 180 in hsk3) | When `accept` builds the intent from a layout, it must print `188 files have no label: not checked` and list them in `accept.json`. This follows the rule "never drop silently", and it is itself a useful finding (stale cache files ship to the site). |
| 7 | `--job summary` duplicates roadmap step 6 | Fold "links land" and "quotes were heard" into step 6's `quote` and `align` commands as their first bench. Do not add a separate `verify` command. Drop the heatmap and laughter footer until step 6 exists. |
| 8 | A dead prior-art link | Replace the Riva 404 with a live source, or delete it. The vllm-omni-mlx issue and the Gradium post already support the ASR round-trip claim. |
| 9 | Adoption depends on a rule the agent may skip | No fix: this is the price. Test 9 already measures it. Keep the kill line, and add the Stop hook only if test 9 fails. Do not add both up front. |

The First move stays as written, with two additions:

- Report every metric split by syllable count (1, 2, 3+).
- Add a fifth bad set: 20 polyphone words (银行, 音乐, 还是, 长大, 重要) rendered by Edge TTS. Record whether any check flags them. The expected result is that nothing does. Record that as the measured blind spot in `.okf/decisions/`.

Runs: `uv run bench/accept/hsk_build.py && uv run bench/accept/score.py` by hand once, with results split by syllable count; later `mise run bench-accept` on every model pin change; `mediasift accept --job clips` run by the agent before "done" on generated clip batches; nothing runs inside `see`.

## Verdict

**Survives with changes.**

The core idea holds. For media the agent made, it already has an exact answer key, and comparing against it costs $0 and adds no tokens to the card. The shift-by-one check is the strongest part: it turns 45 failures into one fault with one fix, and the HSK set is a free real bench for it.

It must carry these changes:

1. Ship one job (`clips`, covering TTS batches and dub lines). Add other jobs only after a second incident.
2. Score candidate labels with teacher-forced likelihood. Do not use free ASR on 1 to 2 syllable items.
3. Report false alarms by syllable count. Gate `rushed` to 4+ syllables. Change the "stuck together" unit to dip depth, or drop it.
4. Seed canaries deterministically and restrict them to eligible items. Exit 3 means only a real judge failure.
5. Print "not checked: tones" in the header. Record the polyphone blind spot as a measured number.
6. Count unlabelled files out loud.
7. Move `summary` into roadmap step 6.

## C03 Speech integrity: underline what was not really heard

## The concept

**For whom.** The agent that reads the card and then quotes it, translates it, or pastes words from it
into a web search. Behind it is the owner: he gets a confident sentence built on a word the video
never said. His jobs are fact-checking a TikTok, summarising and linking talks with times, and
dubbing. The failure in all of them is the same: a wrong token goes downstream as if someone heard it.

**What it is.** Today a `say` line looks the same whether the ASR heard a sentence cleanly, looped on
it 44 times, wrote a YouTube outro over real speech, or dropped half of what was shouted. Several
detectors can each find one of these failures. All of them report the same fact: *this text is not
what was said*. So they share one mark and one footer:

- `~words~` around tokens that two hearings do not agree on;
- `say?` for a span where speech was heard but no words were agreed;
- one footer: `do not quote: ~marked~ words (two hearings disagree), say? spans (heard, not written)`.

The surprising part, kept from the pitch: **the card gets shorter while it stops hiding errors.**
Collapsing a loop removes far more characters than the marks add. And every alarm is first
**re-heard, within a fixed compute budget**, before anything is marked. Most alarms then resolve into
better words, not a hedge. Only what the budget could not settle stays on the card as a `say?` line
priced with a `look`, so nothing is dropped silently.

**What this development changed (measured today, see the table below).**
1. The pitch's flagship example is wrong. Tears of Steel `02:16.1-02:31.6 "It's not my fault, you
   know."` is not missing speech. Blender's official subtitles put that line at 136-138 s, and CED
   hears **no** speech at 140-150 s (Speech-family seconds = 0.0). Parakeet stretched the last token's
   duration to 15.5 s. The same holds for `"Tom."` 03:45.7-03:50.7. A words-per-second rule on
   line durations (m05.6, m08.r1.2) would fire falsely on exactly the two lines the pitch quoted.
   Replace it with **CED speech-seconds vs ASR words per 5 s window**, and clamp each line's end
   time to the end of CED's speech run. The end-time clamp is an integrity fix too: a cited time is
   part of the quote.
2. The real drops on Tears are elsewhere: `03:10-03:15 "Are you ready? Of course you're ready"` and
   `03:28-03:37 "Move your asses! Go go go! I love it! Come on, go!"`. The CED-gap rule fires on both.
3. **Re-hearing the span alone fixes most of them, with no separation.** The dropped words came from
   decoding a long 120 s chunk, not from the sound itself. Parakeet run again on just 188-198 s
   recovers "Are you ready? Of course you're ready." mlx-whisper on 208-222 s writes the official
   subtitle word for word. On 50-56 s both models say "locked" where the card says "luck". So vocal
   separation (m01.8) moves to the last rung, not the first.
4. On the owner's real Vietnamese reel (the `bắt tự` file), the box's whisper-turbo did two things.
   It **replaced 0-20 s of real speech** (CED Speech-family 3.5-5.0 s in every 5 s window) with the
   stock hallucination "Hãy subscribe cho kênh Ghiền Mì Gõ..." three times, and it looped "bắt tự"
   at 25-39 s. That reel has 38 words in 20 s, so a rate check cannot see the hallucination. A known
   phrase list or a second hearing can, and QUAN on the Mac transcribes the same 0-20 s as real
   speech in 10 s. A second VN card in the cache (`track12.mp4`) has the same template ("Hãy
   subscribe cho kênh La La School...") over 30 s, followed by a "음~~" loop.
5. The model's own confidence is no witness. Parakeet's token confidences on Tears are 0.87-1.00 on
   every line, including the wrong "luck" (0.97). This agrees with the literature (Prior art).

## How it works

**1. Alarms (no new model; all from things `see` already computes).** Each alarm becomes one row in an
`integrity` queue in `sensed.json`: span, reason, priority, estimated cost.

| alarm | rule | what it catches (measured) |
|---|---|---|
| loop | an n-gram (1-4 tokens) repeated >= 4 times in a row | "bắt tự" x44 / x40 on the VN reel (2 cards, same file, box whisper) |
| stock phrase | Aho-Corasick over a small pinned bag of known hallucinations ("Hãy subscribe cho kênh … Để không bỏ lỡ những video hấp dẫn", "Thanks for watching", "Untertitel …", "amara.org") | 0-20 s of the VN reel; track12 0-30 s. It is an **alarm, not a filter**: python_100s really says "Thanks for watching", and a re-hearing that agrees clears it |
| heard, not written | per 5 s window: CED Speech-family (classes 0-5, 7, 70) > 0.3 for >= 2 s, and ASR words / speech-seconds < 0.6 | Tears 190-195, 210-220 (real drops), 50-55 (a real substitution), and 0-5, 10-15 (radio chatter and an explosion: unresolved, see below) |
| language switch (phase 2) | whisper-tiny LID per 30 s chunk disagrees with the file language | not measured yet (m05.4) |

The CED Speech-family track has to be **saved** in `sensed.json`, because today only beds are kept. That
is 480 values for 4 minutes and about 6.3k values (~30 KB) for the 1h45 podcast.

**2. One escalation ladder, one budget (m08.r2.4).** Rows are processed by priority: a number or name
inside the span first, then stock phrase, loop, heard-not-written, language. Each rung runs only on
the flagged span with ±1 s of context:

| rung | what | measured cost on Tears spans |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | the same backend again, on the span alone (short context) | parakeet 2.1-2.8 s per span with model load; well under 1 s with the model kept warm |
| R2 | the other backend: mlx-whisper turbo on the Mac (QUAN for `vi`), or the box's faster-whisper when `--on` is up | 0.9-1.0 s per span warm, 4.9 s for the first (load) |
| R3 (only if R1 and R2 both fail and the span sits under a music bed) | vocal stem by separation on the GPU box, then R1 again | not measured; htdemucs on the Mac CPU is ~0.4x realtime (anydub's number) |

Resolution: tokens on which two hearings agree are printed plain, and the original goes to
`sensed.json` (`was: "luck"`). Tokens they disagree on are printed `~like this~`. A span with no agreed
words becomes `say? 00:10-00:15 speech-like sound, no words agreed (look 00:10-00:15)`. A loop prints
once: `~"bắt tự" x44~`. The budget is `max(5 s, 10% of the card's sensing time)` locally, or 60 s
on the box. Rows that do not fit stay `say?` with the price of a `look`. With `MEDIASIFT_FULL_SPEED=1`
the budget is lifted.

**3. On the card.** Tears of Steel would change like this (abridged):

```
00:00.0-00:04.5 say? radio voice, words not agreed (~"Roger, Roger"~ / ~"We have"~)
00:10.0-00:15.0 say? speech-like sound under explosion, no words agreed
00:49.9-00:53.5 say [voice D] "Robot's memory synced and locked."
02:16.1-02:19.2 say [voice E] "It's not my fault, you know."
03:10.2-03:15.0 say "Are you ready? Of course you're ready."
03:28.1-03:37.0 say "Go! ~Move your asses!~ Go, go, go! ~I love it!~ Come on! Go!"
do not quote: ~marked~ words (two hearings disagree); say? spans (heard, not written)
```

The VN reel would change like this: three stock-phrase lines are replaced by QUAN's re-hearing of
0-20 s, about 400 characters of loop collapse into one token, and the footer is added. Net: shorter.

**4. Where it plugs in.** `see.py::_speech` (end-time clamp, alarms); `sound.py::speech_spans` (keep the
track); `asr.py` (a `rehear(pcm, span, backend)` that reuses `transcribe`'s pickers); the card
renderer (marks, footer). Bump `CARD_VERSION` to see-13. A model that is missing (no mlx-whisper on
Linux) logs one stderr line, and its rows stay `say?`. Nothing is dropped.

## What must be true, and the cheapest test

| must be true | cheapest test | status |
|---|---|---|
| CED's Speech family is high where people talk under music and effects, and low on music alone | the Tears windows vs SRT | **holds on 1 film**: zero speech-seconds at 140-150 s (music), 4.5-5.0 s on the shouted spans |
| Short-context re-hearing recovers dropped words | R1/R2 on the flagged Tears spans | **holds on 3 of 3 real-drop spans**; untested on Vietnamese drops |
| Two hearings disagreeing is a usable error signal (not the model's own confidence) | per-token agreement vs SRT on the re-heard spans | partly: the disagreements ("Move your asses"/"Go come") were real errors on one side; n is tiny |
| Agents obey `~` and `say?` (do not search or quote marked tokens) | the 3-version test in First move | **unknown, and it is the main risk.** The editor study below found highlighting did not change correction quality for humans |
| The alarm fires rarely on clean speech | sweep the rule over the saved CED track of every real cached card + the 6 real-footage bench videos; it must stay under ~1 window per 10 minutes of clean talk | not run (the CED track is not saved yet; re-tag costs ~seconds per file) |
| Shared mishearings get caught | — | **does not hold.** QUAN and whisper both write "bắt tự" for "bát tự" (bazi), so a second hearing agrees on the wrong term. Only a caption/OCR witness or a cross-video term base (C-concepts on captions) fixes that. This concept must say so in its footer docs, not claim it |

**The bench the owner reads, made non-gameable.** Keep the pitch's sentence, but count both sides on
the fact-check task over planted-error cards: (a) **marked tokens that reach a search, target 0**, and
(b) **wrong unmarked tokens that reach a search**, which must fall versus the unmarked card. Also cap the
share of tokens marked (< 3% on clean talks). Otherwise a detector that marks everything wins (a)
for free.

## Prior art (with URLs)

- **Whisper hallucinations on non-speech audio, and the "bag of hallucinations".** Barański et al.,
  ICASSP 2025: 40.3% of 301k non-speech inputs produced text, 9.1% of it looping. They remove the
  hallucinations by delooping plus Aho-Corasick over the bag. https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.11378 .
  *Closest prior art.* Differences here: the bag is an **alarm that triggers a re-hearing**, not a
  deletion (python_100s really says "Thanks for watching"); the trigger also covers speech that was
  *dropped*, which a text-only filter cannot see; and the output is a mark for a downstream agent.
- **Harm framing.** Careless Whisper (FAccT 2024): about 1% of transcriptions had wholly hallucinated
  phrases, 38% of those harmful, more often with long non-vocal stretches.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08021
- **The Vietnamese stock hallucination is folklore.** "Hãy subscribe cho kênh Ghiền Mì Gõ" is called
  Whisper's "legendary hallucination" by a Vietnamese ML forum
  (https://www.facebook.com/groups/machinelearningcoban/posts/2424314444692615/), and it appears in
  Netflix's Vietnamese ASR subtitles
  (https://forum.languagelearningwithnetflix.com/t/asr-subtitles-begin-with-speech-from-unrelated-content/18242).
  It shows up in 2 of the owner's own cached files.
- **Highlighting doubtful words in transcript editors** is common: Echo360's confidence slider
  (https://support.echo360.com/hc/en-us/articles/11077346298381), Limecraft
  (https://support.limecraft.com/support/solutions/articles/48001224344), and SyncWords colours
  (https://support.syncwords.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051506131). The evaluation is sobering: with
  E2E ASR confidence, the best per-file thresholds gave precision and recall around 0.5, and
  highlighting did not change how much users improved WER (F(2,70)=0.27, p=.77).
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.15124 . This is why the mark here comes from **two hearings disagreeing**
  and from the CED gap, not from the model's confidence, which was 0.87-1.00 on every Tears line.
  It is also why the obedience test comes before the footer.
- **Separation before ASR helps on music** (lyrics transcription with Whisper, where separation also
  serves as vocal activity detection): https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15514 . This supports R3, but on Tears
  the drops were recovered without it.
- **Detection from Whisper internals** (SAE and steering, 2026): https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07473 . It
  needs model internals and does not carry over to QUAN or parakeet; not pursued.
- Reference used for the measurements: Blender's official Tears of Steel English subtitles,
  https://download.blender.org/demo/movies/ToS/subtitles/TOS-en.srt .
- Local facts: `parakeet_mlx` 0.5.2 exposes per-token entropy confidence (`alignment.py`).
  faster-whisper 1.2.1 `BatchedInferencePipeline.transcribe` defaults to `vad_filter=True` and
  `compression_ratio_threshold=2.4`, and it still looped on the box. The two box cards of the same
  file loop a different number of times (x44 vs x40), so box decoding is not deterministic.

`Not researched:` per-chunk language-ID accuracy on Vietnamese-English code-switching, and the cost of
whisper-tiny run per 30 s chunk. Risk: the phase-2 language alarm may be too noisy or cost more than
the 5 s floor allows.

## What it costs to run

- **$/month and quotas:** 0. Local models that are already pinned and installed; no API.
- **Per card, compute:** the alarms are a regex plus array arithmetic over the CED scores that are
  already computed (~0 s). Escalation is capped at `max(5 s, 10%)` of sensing time locally, or 60 s on
  the box. On Tears, cold calls for all five spans summed to ~20 s; with warm models it is about 5-8 s
  (estimate). The cap therefore binds on short films and leaves the lowest-priority rows as `say?`.
  Peak memory: R2's mlx-whisper turbo runs after the picture pass, like today's speech, so it does not
  stack on the 3.3 GB MLX peak.
- **Per card, tokens:** a card where nothing fires costs +0 tokens (no footer when there is nothing to
  report). On Tears it is about +60 tokens on ~1.9k (3%). On a looping or hallucinating card it is net
  negative (about -100 tokens on the VN reel). Each fixed span also saves the agent a `look` (~1.8k
  tokens) it would otherwise need to check.
- **Storage:** the CED Speech track in `sensed.json`, ≤ ~30 KB for a 1h45 file.
- **Operational:** a missing second backend (Linux has no MLX) or an unreachable box means one stderr
  line, and those rows stay `say?`. Nothing pages anyone. The see-13 version bump re-senses cached cards
  on their next hit: minutes for a long talk, once.
- **Maintenance:** one pinned stock-phrase bag (grows only from phrases seen on real cards, each with a
  test), three thresholds tuned on the integrity bench, one `rehear()` that must keep working with
  `pick()`. The obedience test must be re-run when the card format changes.
- **Runs:** adds the integrity queue inside `see` (bounded by the budget), the saved CED Speech track,
  and `bench/sense/integrity.py`. No new service, model download or daemon.

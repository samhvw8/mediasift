## Builds
### m02.r2.1 Cut the link everyone leans on: the burned-in caption is not an independent witness
Builds on: m02.r1.1 + m03.r1.2 + m05.r1.2 + m05.2
Three of us treat the on-screen caption as a second, independent hearing of the speech, but most VN and EN reels caption with CapCut or TikTok auto-captions, which are themselves ASR, so the caption and our ASR can share the same mishearing ("bắt tự" in both) and their agreement would then prove nothing. Build the version that survives this: before any caption earns witness status, measure caption-ASR error correlation on reels with a hand-checked transcript; if errors co-occur well above chance, label that caption `auto-caption (same kind of witness as ASR)` and count caption+ASR agreement as one witness, not two, and only a hand-edited caption (it differs from a raw ASR pass on fillers and line breaks) or platform subtitles uploaded by the creator count as independent. Cheapest settling test: 20 real reels, one person listens to the 5 number/name tokens in each, and we count how often caption and ASR are wrong together; under 1 in 5 of the shared-wrong rate needed to matter, the three parents stand as written.

### m02.r2.2 Every self-growing bench prints its months-to-verdict before it ships
Builds on: m04.r1.3 + m05.r1.3 + m04.r1.5 + m02.r1.3
The self-grading loops (YouTube chapters, harvested side data, outcome rows that delete unused fields) all assume real use will supply enough rows, but real use is about 7 non-bench cards a month, and telling 70% from 80% needs on the order of 150 rows. Add a sample-size clock: each loop declares its expected rows per month, measured from the last 30 days of session logs (e.g. how many real URLs actually had chapters), and `mediasift bench report` prints `chapter finder: 3 rows, verdict at ±10 pp in ~40 months`; a loop whose clock exceeds 6 months must ship with a synthetic or public proxy bench as its judge, with the real rows kept as a sanity check only. The same clock gates m04.r1.5's auto-deletion: a field cannot be removed for "moving no outcome in N runs" unless N clears the power bar, or a lucky streak deletes a useful line. Cheapest test: one log query, count of real URLs with chapters, subtitles or a description track in the last month.

### m02.r2.3 The dub loop may only remix on a detector with a measured miss rate
Builds on: m04.r1.1 + m02.r1.4 + m04.3
m04.r1.1 lets `check DUB --against SOURCE` trigger remixes automatically, which makes the detector's errors act on the owner's files; two of its claims were never observed: that CED hears a bed under a loud dub voice, and that dub WER against anydub's own script tracks "sounds like English read aloud". Gate the loop on two calibrations: run check on source-vs-source and on source-vs-sabotaged mixes (bed removed, bed at -10/-20/-30 dB, voice synthesised with an English-trained TTS) to get a hit and false-alarm rate per check, and only checks with false alarms under 1 in 20 may auto-remix; the rest only print. The WER-as-accent proxy gets the cheapest possible test first: the dubs the owner complained about (09-23, 09-25) against dubs he accepted; if WER does not separate them, it is dropped before it is built.

### m02.r2.4 Every footage or audio match prints its chance-match rate from a negative control
Builds on: m03.r1.3 + m04.r1.2 + m03.5
The morgue's "audio first sensed in X" and the repost detector's "re-voiced copy of card Y" are claims of identity, and a 32x18 colour-grid hash will collide on black frames, title cards and studio talking heads; with 730 near-identical bench clips in the cache, a false link is the likely first output. Before any match line ships, run the matcher across all pairs of known-unrelated cached cards to get the false-match rate per hash length and shot duration, then print it beside each hit (`footage matches card Y 00:03-01:10; chance match in this cache under 1 in 2000`) and suppress shots that are mostly uniform or text. The split tell (audio matches, shots do not) gets the same control, using bench clips that share stock music. Cost: one offline sweep over the existing cache.

### m02.r2.5 A hedge is only worth its tokens if the agent obeys it: plant false hedges
Builds on: m05.r1.2 + m03.r1.2 + m02.r1.3
The link everyone assumes from card to answer is that an agent which reads `do not quote verbatim: 00:12-00:31` or `"15|50 percent"` will change what it does; nobody has observed that. Cheapest test: take 10 real cards, make three versions (no hedge, true hedge, hedge on a span that is actually correct), give each to a fresh agent with a fact-check or summary task, and count quoted or web-searched hedged tokens; if the true hedge does not cut bad quotes, or the false hedge costs nothing either, the agent is ignoring the line and the hedge format, not the detector, is what needs work. This is the rent test for every warning-style line the council proposes, it runs in an afternoon on cards already cached, and it separates "the sensor is wrong" from "the card is not read".

### m02.r2.6 (fresh) The music splice detector needs a negative control on unedited music
Builds on: m03.r1.5 + m06.1
A beat-phase jump or level step inside one `say` line is read as an audio edit, but film and social scores change tempo, drop out and swell on their own, and a music-ducking compressor moves bed level with every word. Before the line can say `audio edited here`, run the detector on a set of unedited single-take talks with continuous beds (the owner's own long talks, plus bench music under TTS speech with no splice) and measure its false-flag rate per hour; ship only if it stays under one per hour while catching the scripted splices from m03.2's bench. Below that bar, print the weaker `music discontinuity at 00:12.4` without the word "edited", so the agent sees the evidence without a verdict the data cannot support.

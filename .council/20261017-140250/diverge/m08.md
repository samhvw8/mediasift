## Baseline (the obvious answers)
- Put vocal sounds on the speech line they overlap (`say [laughing] "..."`) so the reader does not have to join snd and say lines itself.
- Add a chapter / topic summary for long talks so summarise-with-timestamps jobs start from structure, not from 1,500 speech lines.
- Call the on-demand audio LLM on the GPU box for one span when the card is not enough.

Reading I picked: "what the agent pays" = tokens of the card plus the images it opens, plus turns wasted on wrong instructions. Token counts below are chars/4 (English; Vietnamese costs more per char), image tokens are w*h/750. All numbers were measured today on the 734 cards in `~/.cache/mediasift/see/`, `usage.jsonl` and Claude Code session logs; nothing new was built.

## Assumptions I broke
- "The card is already lean; the token risk is adding lines": the median card is 43% fixed header (~950 chars, ~240 tokens) that repeats SKILL.md. On the 15 s YouTube clip the header is 978 of 1,035 chars (94%), and on the 26 s reels it is 52%. On the owner's real job type (a 47-min Vietnamese talk, `altman_vi`), 84 `pic` lines take ~12.6k of 23k chars (55%, ~3k tokens), and 82 of them are OCR echoes of one title card ("wI", "ite", "HoW", "*Write"). The rules say a line must earn its place before it is added, but nothing makes a line keep earning it. The cheapest win is deleting text, not adding sensors.
- "Cost means card text": one `ev_NN.jpg` (1536x864) costs ~1.8k tokens, about as much as the whole Tears of Steel card (7.3k chars, ~1.9k tokens). A `flow` sheet (1488x465) costs ~0.9k. Bench round r15 opened 55 images on 4 videos. The agent cannot see these prices, so it cannot budget.

## Ideas
### m08.1 The cached card tells every agent to run a command that no longer exists
733 of the 734 cached cards say `framesift look ...`, and `framesift` is not on PATH. The rename kept `CARD_VERSION = "see-12"`, so the cache key did not change, and every cache hit since the rename (104 so far in usage.jsonl) prints the dead command. The agent pays one failed turn, then has to guess. Fix it now: render the "How to use this card" header when the card is printed instead of storing it in `card.md`, and cut it to one line that points at the skill. That also removes ~240 tokens from every card. Judge: grep the cache for a dead command name (it should find 0), and rerun the synthetic and real benches with the one-line header (same score, fewer tokens).
### m08.2 Tokens per correct answer as the bench's second column, then a rent audit of every line kind
The benches already count images and keep the sub-agents' usage reports. Add card tokens plus image tokens per question, and report "correct per 10k tokens" next to "correct". Then ablate one line kind at a time on the kept rounds (the CUTS list, camera moves, OCR text, `+N smaller changes`, the Sensed line). Any kind whose removal costs 0 questions goes, or is printed only on request. This is the missing deletion gate. It is the counterpart of "a feature without a judge cannot earn a line", and it needs no new data.
### m08.3 An ASR loop guard: never print a hallucinated loop as speech
In a real phone video the owner downloaded (`v14044g...MP4`, Vietnamese), the card prints "bắt tự, " 44 times (~500 chars) and "Hãy subscribe cho kênh ..." three times in one line. Worse, "bắt tự" is a mishearing of "bát tự" (bazi). A fact-check agent would search the wrong term and also pay for the loop. Collapse a repeated n-gram to `"bắt tự" (x44, ASR repeated itself here: likely a decoding loop, check with look)`. That flags the corruption instead of passing it off as speech, which follows the crash-before-corrupt rule. Judge: a regex sweep of the cache found 3 loops in 2 cards; a synthetic clip with a looped line is the test.
### m08.4 Recurring on-screen text becomes one line, not 80
Group OCR reads across events by fuzzy match, and print text that recurs as one line: `on screen again and again: "How I Write" (title card, 80 times 00:13-47:10)`. Drop fragments under ~4 letters that match nothing ("m o", "ng có", "Til"). On `altman_vi` this alone saves ~3k tokens per read. On TikToks the recurring text is often the creator handle (`@tu.phu.ngo triet`), which is exactly what the owner's "script with credit to its author" job needs. Shown once, at the top, it becomes more useful than it is now as scattered fragments.
### m08.5 Put a price tag on every image
List images as `flow_03.jpg (00:23-00:33, ~0.9k tok)` and `ev_01.jpg (~1.8k tok, 3 events)`. The agent decides from the card which picture to open, and today an event sheet looks as cheap as a text line. Next, cap event sheets near 1100 px on the long edge. The 320 px tile cut already showed a precedent: 44% fewer tokens at the same accuracy. Judge: images opened and image tokens per question on the real bench, with the score held.
### m08.6 Stop repeating what the footer already says on every speech line
`[voice D, male]` is repeated on every line, and the footer already says D is male. Contiguous speech repeats each end time as the next line's start. In the Tears card, voice tags are 428 chars and timestamps 1,200 of the 5,600 timeline chars. Print the bare letter, and print an end time only when there is a gap after the line. Most of the cost is in the speech-heavy cards (podcasts, talks), which are the owner's main use. Judge: the synthetic sync and order questions must hold.
### m08.7 Long talks: replace the 300-entry CUTS list with a camera-angle summary
`altman_vi` lists 300 cuts letter by letter (truncated at +240), and `cancer.mp4` does the same. For a multi-camera interview the useful fact is "300 cuts among 5 angles (E-I alternate: an interview filmed from several cameras)", in one line. The full list stays in `sensed.json` for the rare question that needs a single cut. This applies only above a cut count, so short videos, where a cut is an event, keep their list.
### m08.8 Find out what agents actually skip from logs already on disk
`usage.jsonl` has 1,074 lines: 909 senses, 104 cache hits, 61 `look` zooms. The session logs outside the bench show 4 sessions, 7 `see` calls, 4 flow-sheet reads, 0 `look` calls and 0 reads of `transcript.txt`. A small `bench/usage.py` could join the two and report, per card, which images and files were opened after `see`. That would give the rent audit in m08.2 a real-use prior at zero runtime cost, instead of guessing from bench agents alone. (The real-use sample is thin today. Say so in the report rather than tuning to it.)

## The provocation I would put to the group
The next extension should be a deletion with a number on it. In the owner's real videos, more than half of what the reading agent pays for today is boilerplate, OCR echo and an ASR loop, so make every card line keep earning its place before mediasift learns to sense anything new.

## Baseline (the obvious answers)
- Build the five ideas on the table: vocal sounds on the speech line, speaker-relative prosody, mood corrections through `fix`, a music build/peak finder, and an audio LLM on the GPU box for one span.
- A "linked videos" mode: index several cards, then cite and translate across them with timestamps.
- Better Vietnamese and short-vertical-video support: a VN speech bench, captions burned into reels, and TikTok/Facebook fetching.

## Assumptions I broke
- "The failure a year from now will be a wrong line on the card": it will more likely be a card nobody notices is stale, unused or bypassed. Evidence from today: 733 of 734 cached cards still tell the agent to run `framesift look "..."`, a command that is no longer on PATH. They will keep saying it for as long as the cache lives, because the cache key holds `CARD_VERSION` but not the instructions. The `fix` loop that ideas (3) and the mood plan depend on has never run: `~/.cache/mediasift/feedback.jsonl` does not exist.
- "A bench number proves it helps the owner": of 1013 `see` calls, about 730 are bench runs on AudioSet clips, synthetic clips and Blender films. Real use is about 7 cards: Vietnamese reels, a crash recording and long talks. Every bench score can rise for a year while the owner's actual jobs (fact-checking a VN TikTok, linking talks) get no better, and nothing would show it.

## Ideas
### m05.1 The failure ledger mined from session logs (replaces `fix`)
What happened by October 2027: `fix` was used zero times, as it had been in the whole first month, so the mood test set and the corrections loop never grew. Every heuristic stayed tuned to AudioSet. Instead, a monthly offline script reads the Claude Code transcripts (`~/.claude/projects/*.jsonl`) for traces of a card failing: three or more `look` calls on one span, the agent falling back to raw `yt-dlp`, `whisper` or anydub, the owner writing "no, he says..." or re-pasting the same URL, and the agent quoting words that are not in the card. Each hit becomes one row (video key, time, what was wrong), and those rows become the real-footage bench. No runtime cost and no agent behaviour to rely on; it is the only judge built from the owner's actual jobs.

### m05.2 Two-witness speech for fact-checks: numbers and names only
What happened: the owner fact-checked a TikTok, and the agent web-searched a figure that the ASR had mis-heard ("15%" for "50%", or a misspelled name). The search "disproved" the video, and the owner reposted the correction. Many VN and EN reels burn their captions into the picture, OCR already reads them, and URL fetch already pulls platform subtitles. When the speech line and the caption or subtitle disagree on a number, a proper noun or a negation, mark only that token (`"50% [caption: 15%]"`). When they agree, add nothing, so the card stays short. Reels with burned-in captions label themselves, so the judge comes free: collect 50 of them and score how many disagreements are true ASR errors.

### m05.3 Cache what was sensed; render the instructions at read time
What happened: the rename already did it once. Every cached card points at a dead `framesift look` and a dead path, and the next move, rename or flag change will do it again without anyone noticing, because a cache hit never re-renders. Split `card.md` into a cached timeline plus a header (zoom command, sheet list, voices note) that the current binary rewrites on every `see`, including on a hit. Judge: a test that renames the binary and the sample path and asserts that a cached card prints neither old string. This removes a whole class of silent staleness at zero runtime cost.

### m05.4 A tripwire for language switching
What happened: `transcribe()` detects the language once per file and `pick()` routes to one backend. VN reels mix in English phrases, brand names and hooks, and the English stretches came out as plausible Vietnamese syllables (or the reverse). The agent translated them with confidence. Fix: run language ID per 30 s chunk, which is the same detector on shorter input. When a chunk disagrees with the file, either re-run that chunk on the other backend or write one line, `(00:42-01:10 sounded English; transcribed as Vietnamese)`. Judge: stitch VN and EN clips with known boundaries, then measure WER per chunk before and after.

### m05.5 Make tokens per answered job the meter, not lines per card
What happened: every card stayed short and every feature earned its line. But "link these 4 talks with timestamps" cost the agent about 150k tokens each time, because it re-read four `transcript.txt` files and grepped and re-read them again. That bill went to the agent's session, so mediasift's own numbers never showed it. Measure it from the same session logs as m05.1: card tokens, plus transcript bytes read, plus `look` images, per real job. Make that the number a feature has to lower. It may show that the cheapest win is a per-video ledger of quotable lines, not another sensor.

### m05.6 Say where speech went missing instead of dropping it
What happened: this exists on today's Tears of Steel card. `02:16.1-02:31.6 say "It's not my fault, you know."` gives 5 words over 15.5 s, and the last line is `"The the"`. The rest of what was said is gone without a word, which breaks the "never drop speech silently" rule while every bench stays green. Fix: when a say line's words per second falls far below the speaker's rate, or CED hears speech in a window that ASR left empty, add `(speech heard, not transcribed, 02:18-02:31)`. The agent then knows to `look` or to hedge. Judge: synthetic clips with scripted gaps, plus the dialogue bench, scoring the flag's precision and recall.

### m05.7 Failures that say what to do, not just that they failed
What happened: by month four the TikTok and Facebook extractors in yt-dlp broke twice, and Facebook reels needed a login. The GPU box sat in Windows for weeks, so 1h45 podcasts ran as MLX on the Mac and the owner stopped sending them. Each failure raised an error or fell back correctly, and the agent then "fact-checked" the post's caption text instead of the video. The owner never learned the video was never heard. Make every URL or backend failure end with an instruction line for the agent ("the video was not sensed; do not describe its content; fix: `mise up yt-dlp`" or "try `--cookies-from-browser chrome`"). On a fallback, print the time it cost into the card header (`speech ran locally: GPU box unreachable, +9 min`). Judge: a test per failure path that asserts the instruction line is present.

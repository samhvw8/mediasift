# Stress test: C05 The creator's burned-in captions become the speech track and the free judge

Checked locally before attacking: `_drop_captions` in `src/mediasift/see.py:370` (deletes OCR text
sharing >= 60% of 3+-letter words with speech within 6 s); `asr.pick` and `_early_audio` in
`asr.py` / `see.py`; the owner's shell (`MEDIASIFT_ON=<gpu-box>`); `_fetch` in `see.py:76-98`
(URL inputs already run `yt-dlp --write-subs --write-auto-subs`); the four cited cards (all exist; all
four were made with `faster-whisper@<gpu-box>`, not QUAN); `.okf/decisions/speech-and-speed.md`
(OCR ~51 ms per accurate read, OCR is the slowest local pass).

## Fragile assumptions

Ranked most fragile first.

1. **Captions are an independent witness, so their disagreement with ASR means something. UNTESTED,
   and probably false for a large share of the owner's input.** The one data point (`bát tự` vs
   `bắt tự` on c264) is n = 1. CapCut Auto Caption and TikTok's own caption tool are speech-to-text
   ([CapCut help](https://www.capcut.com/help/how-to-recognise-subtitles); TikTok's editor "generates
   captions automatically on upload and lets you edit them",
   [convertaudiototext.com](https://convertaudiototext.com/blog/captioning-for-tiktok-instagram)). An
   unedited auto-caption is a third ASR, and on Vietnamese social video it is likely a weaker one than
   large-v3-turbo. The concept does gate on this, which is to its credit, but the gate *is* the
   product: without it, the inversion (`say (captions)`) does not ship and the near-miss bracket
   prints two spellings with no way to pick. The "independence check" from surface signs (fillers,
   casing, digits, line breaks) is itself unjudged: CapCut writes digits and drops fillers too, so the
   signs that a human edited it are also signs of a different ASR's normaliser.

2. **The contradiction bucket catches hallucinations that the default path produces. SHAKY.** The
   motivating failure (outro ghost text, a 40-fold `bắt tự` loop) is a whisper failure mode
   ([whisper-ghosts](https://github.com/jcpsimmons/whisper-ghosts)). The concept's own "first move"
   says to re-sense with "today's default (QUAN)". But QUAN is a CTC reader and CTC does not invent an
   outro sentence over silence; it deletes (152 deletions vs 22 on the talk, `speech-and-speed.md`).
   And the owner's real default is *neither* what the concept says: with `MEDIASIFT_ON` set,
   `_early_audio` sends Vietnamese to faster-whisper on the box whenever it is up, and QUAN runs only
   when the box is off or in Windows. So the contradiction bucket matters on one backend (box whisper)
   and is close to idle on the other (QUAN). Its value depends on a routing fact the concept got wrong,
   and a bench run on QUAN only will undersell it, while a run on whisper only will oversell it.

3. **Vision reads real caption fonts well enough for a syllable-level compare. UNTESTED on real
   footage.** Synthetic Arial/Helvetica at 28-40 px gave 0.3-1.4% CER, but already showed the exact
   error class that matters most: a tone mark (`đâu` -> `đầu`). The near-miss bucket is defined as
   "differing tokens are a name, a number or a tone mark"; an OCR tone-mark slip lands *in that bucket*
   and is printed as "the creator's spelling". Real TikTok captions are stylised (yellow highlight,
   per-word colour pop, stroke, emoji, 576x1024 recompressed), and the c264 card shows today's
   changed-region OCR only got fragments (`"ng có"`). The one public comparable reports ~5% character
   error for OCR of clean burned-in Chinese subtitles on a VOD and only ~58% speech coverage
   ([zkaelxz/U00#1022](https://github.com/zkaelxz/U00/pull/1022)). 5% CER in Vietnamese is roughly
   one wrong syllable in every 4-5 words, well past the concept's 3% kill line.

Solid, for the record: zero running cost in $ and models (no new download, Vision already loaded);
captions are common in the owner's reels (3 of 4, n small but consistent with the genre); prior art
claims check out ([submerge](https://github.com/rixwoodling/submerge) does OCR+Whisper fusion for
proper nouns and fewer hallucinations, and nothing found turns the disagreement into agent-facing
output or a bench).

**Missed entirely by the concept:** for URL inputs, `_fetch` already pulls site subtitles, and when
any exist they *replace* ASR (`backend: subtitles`, ASR never runs). yt-dlp's TikTok extractor pulls
`auto_video_caption_info`, `cla_info.caption_infos`, `subtitleInfos` and a `creator_caption` format
([yt-dlp tiktok.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/master/yt_dlp/extractor/tiktok.py)).
So for a TikTok link, the say track may already be TikTok's ASR, and C05 would align burned-in
captions against *another platform's ASR*, not ours. The "not researched" note in the concept treats
this as a future shortcut; it is a present-day behaviour that changes what every bucket means.

## Pre-mortem

October 2027. `captions.py` shipped behind the gate, the gate never opened, and nobody noticed.

The first-move script ran in an afternoon and looked good on c264, because c264 was a whisper card with
a loop. The owner did the 10 + 10 listening once. The 20-reel independence test needed 20 captioned
reels with 5 hand-checked tokens each, and real captioned reels arrive at maybe one or two a week, so
it stalled at 7. With the gate closed, the card printed `[heard: ...]` brackets with both spellings
and the header line `captions: burned-in, vi, agree with speech 91%`. The agent reading the card did
not know which spelling to trust either, so it used whichever came first, which was the ASR line, as
before. Most reels went through QUAN on days the box was in Windows, where the contradiction bucket
was nearly always empty. The other days, TikTok links came with site subtitles, ASR never ran, and the
module aligned two captions against each other and reported 97% "agreement with speech" that was
really TikTok agreeing with CapCut.

Meanwhile the band OCR became the second-slowest pass on long talks (a lower-third name strap read as
a "caption band" on a podcast), and one macOS update shifted Vision's Vietnamese tone marks, which
quietly moved near-miss counts. The `bench/captions.py` cross-cache set existed, but with no one
listening to settle disagreements it was agreement numbers without a truth, so it never became the
ASR bench it promised. Net: one more module, three thresholds and a number normaliser to keep up, a
header line on every captioned card, and the card's behaviour for the agent nearly unchanged.

## Who loses

- **The card's token budget and the reading agent.** Every near-miss bracket and the header line cost
  tokens on every captioned reel, and if the gate stays closed they buy ambiguity rather than an
  answer. The agent's response would be to ignore brackets, which is the silent failure the rules ban.
- **The OCR pass's time budget.** OCR is already the slowest local pass. A second, targeted read
  pattern competes with the existing changed-region OCR for Vision and the Neural Engine, the same
  contention that pushed one run to 150 s (`speech-and-speed.md`). The owner's "lightweight" rule loses
  a little on every captioned video, and more on long videos where a lower-third gets mistaken for a band.
- **The QUAN / box routing decision.** If C05's numbers show box whisper hallucinating where QUAN does
  not, the honest response is to route Vietnamese to QUAN even when the box is up, which makes C05's
  contradiction bucket mostly redundant. C05 succeeding as a *judge* may make C05-as-a-*fixer* pointless,
  and that is fine, but the concept does not plan for that outcome.
- **The owner's listening time.** Every gate in the concept ends in "the owner listens". That is a
  recurring human cost, and the owner is the only Vietnamese judge.
- **The creator, mildly.** Creators bend spellings to dodge moderation (`Tử Ui`); a "correct toward
  captions" pass would repeat their evasions as the spoken word. The concept already guards this.

## Fixes

| weakness | smallest fix |
|---|---|
| Independence untested; gate may never open | Do not build the inversion or the near-miss bracket until the independence test is done. Ship order: (a) offline bench only, (b) standalones + contradiction line, (c) inversion only if co-errors are below a stated number. Make the test cheaper: use the 4 cached reels plus the next real reels and stop at the first 10, not 20; record "unknown" in `.okf/` if it stalls, and treat that as a "no". |
| Wrong routing assumption (QUAN vs box whisper) | Run the first-move bench on **both** backends (box faster-whisper and QUAN) for the same reels, and report contradiction counts per backend. If QUAN's contradiction count is near zero, scope the contradiction line to whisper backends and say so in the header. |
| URL inputs already use site subtitles instead of ASR | Branch on `asr_info.backend`: when it is `subtitles`, either skip C05, or (better, and cheap) treat site subs as the third witness and compare all three. At minimum the header must not say "agree with speech" when there was no speech recognition. Run one `yt-dlp --list-subs` on a captioned TikTok to see whether `creator_caption` is the creator's typed text (which would make band OCR unnecessary for links). |
| OCR tone-mark errors land in the near-miss bucket as "creator's spelling" | Exclude tone-mark-only differences from near-miss until the real-font OCR test shows tone-mark error below a stated rate; count them in the bench instead. Require the same caption read on 2 consecutive band reads (captions persist 0.5-3 s) before a token is used, which costs nothing extra at 2 fps and filters single-frame OCR flips. |
| Real-font OCR unmeasured, prior art shows ~5% CER | Keep the concept's kill line (3% syllable error) and run it first; it is the cheapest test and gates everything else. |
| Lower-thirds, name straps and lyrics mistaken for a band on long videos | Only search for a band when the video is vertical or under ~5 min (the owner's real captioned input); skip on long talks. This also removes the long-video cost line. |
| Card grows with brackets the agent cannot act on | One bracket per reel at most until a sense-bench question shows a gain; the header line only when at least one non-echo bucket is non-empty. |
| Owner listening is the only judge | No fix: this is the price. There is no other Vietnamese judge; keep the asks to one 30-minute session per milestone. |
| Vision drift after macOS updates | Already in the concept (record Vision revision in `Sensed:`); no further change. |

## Verdict

**Survives with changes.** The core idea is sound and cheap: stop deleting caption text blindly, count
it, keep the never-spoken text, and flag lines the caption flatly contradicts. That half earns its way
with no new model. The inversion (`say (captions)`) and the near-miss spelling fix should not ship on
the current evidence. Required changes:

1. Bench on both box faster-whisper and QUAN, because `MEDIASIFT_ON` makes box whisper the owner's real
   default for Vietnamese, not QUAN.
2. Handle URL inputs where site subtitles already replaced ASR (`backend: subtitles`), including
   TikTok's own caption tracks that yt-dlp already pulls.
3. Run the real-font OCR test first; keep tone-mark-only differences out of the near-miss bucket until
   it passes; require two agreeing band reads per token.
4. Limit band search to short or vertical video.
5. Ship in stages: bench, then standalones + contradictions, then the inversion only after the
   independence test passes. If that test stalls, record it as a "no".

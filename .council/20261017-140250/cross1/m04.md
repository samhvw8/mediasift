## Builds
### m04.r1.1 The dub that checks itself before the owner presses play
Builds on: m04.3 + m01.1 + m06.3 + m01.7
The source card becomes both the mix spec and the answer key: m06.3's measured "music under dialogue at -16 dB" per cue and m01.7's per-letter syllable rate are written into `cues.json`, anydub mixes to them, and `mediasift check DUB --against SOURCE` re-measures the same numbers on the dub (bed offset per cue, lost `snd` events, dub WER against anydub's own script, dub rate against the source speaker's rate). If a cue misses by more than a set margin, anydub remixes that span with the measured offset and checks again, at most twice, and only then says "done" with the residual misses listed. What this adds: the owner's two literal complaints ("English too quiet, background lost" and "Vietnamese read like English") are caught and fixed in a loop no human has to hear, and every pass/fail is a free labelled row because the source is ground truth.

### m04.r1.2 Credit block and repost detector from three free signals
Builds on: m04.2 + m06.4 + m06.5
Keep yt-dlp's info JSON (uploader, upload date, canonical URL, `track`/`artist`, "original sound - @handle") and the per-cue and per-tile fingerprints, and print a ready credit block the owner can paste into a shared script: `by @x, posted 2026-10-14; sound: "Track" - Artist`. Then cross the three: when the footage tiles match a cached card but the voice track differs, or the "original sound" handle is not the uploader, the card says `likely re-voiced copy of card Y (00:03-01:10)` or `sound from @other`. What this adds: the 10-15 fact-check finding (an uncredited re-voiced Bilibili copy), which the agent found by eye, becomes one line, and every repost the owner meets is a new judge row for free.

### m04.r1.3 Every YouTube video he summarises grades the chapter finder by itself
Builds on: m06.2 + m04.4 + m04.7
"Summarise with timestamps" is the owner's most repeated ask, and YouTube chapters are published ground truth that yt-dlp already returns. Whenever a real `see` runs on a URL that has chapters, mediasift silently scores its own sting/bed-edge/topic-shift boundaries against them and appends one row to `~/.cache/mediasift/selfgrade.jsonl`; the card only states chapter candidates once that running score passes a bar. The same boundaries become the anchor points for m04.7's cross-card links (`also in: card Z, chapter 3`). What this adds, pushed past m06.2's three podcasts: the bench is not built once, it grows with every talk the owner actually watches, in Vietnamese and English, so the feature earns or loses its line on his real diet rather than a curated set.

### m04.r1.4 Fact-check warnings that learn which claims he actually checked
Builds on: m04.6 + m01.5 + m06.6
m01.5's OCR-vs-ASR match labels on-screen text `standalone`, and m06.6's VO-vs-on-camera test says whether the claim is narrated over stock footage; together they fill m04.6's job footer: `for fact-check: 3 on-screen numbers never spoken; narration over stock footage 00:05-00:40`. The self-service part: when the agent then web-searches during the same session, the hook logs which card spans its search queries came from, so over time mediasift learns which warnings led to a real check and drops footer kinds that never do. What this adds: the footer is pruned by use instead of by guesswork, which keeps the card short without anyone deciding by hand.

### m04.r1.5 Consumers write back, and unused fields delete themselves
Builds on: m01.6 + m06.8 + m04.4
`cues.json` and the M1/M2 cue block only stay honest if someone measures whether each column changes an outcome. Every consumer appends an outcome row keyed by card and field: anydub logs lag, lines over slot and the m04.r1.1 check result; the prompt hook logs owner complaints that name a field ("background lost" maps to ducking, "it dubbed the song" maps to the keep-list). A field or column that has not moved any outcome in N real runs is flagged for removal in `mediasift bench report`. What this adds, ten times larger than "only fields that earned a bench number": fields must keep earning, so the spotting list and cue block shrink as well as grow, and the owner's complaint lands on the exact column that failed.

### m04.r1.6 (fresh) The "what did you still have to check" question, asked once per job
Builds on: m04 provocation + m01 provocation
After a session that used `see` ends with the agent saying done, the hook asks the owner nothing; instead it reads his next message in that thread for a correction or a self-made check (a screenshot he sends, a "nghe lại thì...", a manual link) and tags it with the job kind (dub, fact-check, summary, reel credit, bug repro). A monthly `mediasift gaps` lists the human checks per job ranked by count, which is the backlog of what to sense next, in his own words. What this adds: the council's next round of ideas is chosen by the owner's remaining manual work, measured, instead of by what a lens finds interesting.
